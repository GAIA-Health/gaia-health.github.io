Tracking after periods stop: sleep, hot flashes, heart, bone and mood.

See how it all connects

After menopause, sleep, hot flashes, mood, heart, and bone health all matter, and they usually live in different places. Veya connects your symptom log, wearable sleep and HRV, and lab results, then looks for patterns in your own data. Your history carries over from earlier stages, so you don't start from scratch. Read the menopause guide for background, or how hormones move across a cycle to see what changes when cycles end.
