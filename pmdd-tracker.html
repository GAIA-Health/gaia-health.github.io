Why the chart matters

There's no blood test for PMDD. Diagnosis comes from prospective daily symptom ratings across at least two consecutive cycles, logged as you go rather than recalled afterward, since day-to-day records are far more reliable than memory. Go Go Gaia keeps your mood, symptoms, sleep, and cycle in one place, so after two cycles you can see whether the pattern is really there and bring something concrete to your appointment, instead of describing a hard week from memory.
