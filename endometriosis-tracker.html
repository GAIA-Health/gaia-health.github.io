An endometriosis tracking app for pain, flares, and cycle phase

Endometriosis pain doesn't always look like period pain — it can show up as painful sex, pain with bowel movements, pelvic pressure, back pain, or stomach pain, on any day of your cycle. Go Go Gaia logs each of those by type and severity, keeps cycle tracking running alongside your symptoms, and lets you add medications, treatments, and surgeries over time. When it's time for an appointment, export a doctor-ready record built from what you've actually logged.

Built for how endo actually shows up

Turning on endometriosis in onboarding or Settings — no diagnosis required — brings up this dashboard, and a short endo check-in unlocks the deeper pattern cards below.

Cycle tracking runs alongside your symptoms

Endometriosis pain doesn't only show up with your period; it can appear before bleeding starts, at ovulation, during sex, or with a bowel movement. Go Go Gaia tracks your cycle phase right alongside your pain logs, so your symptoms show up in the context of where you are in your cycle instead of an assumption that they'll match up.

Sleep shows up in your pattern history

Once you've logged a handful of flare episodes and built up some baseline sleep history, Go Go Gaia can start showing you whether your flares tend to follow shorter-sleep nights — from your watch or ring, or from sleep you log yourself. It's a pattern that emerges as your history grows, not something you'll see on day one.

Pain logged the way it actually shows up

Log pain by type and severity: painful sex, pain with bowel movements, pelvic pain or pressure, back pain, and stomach pain, plus diarrhea and constipation. Real vocabulary instead of a single yes-or-no entry.

Built for the appointment, not just the data

Endo appointments are often short, and details are easy to lose track of once you're in the room. The doctor-prep export pulls together your logged symptoms and any change in severity since you started a treatment, into one record built for that conversation.

Surgery and treatment history, in one timeline

Add surgeries, diagnosis dates, and when a treatment started, and Go Go Gaia keeps that history next to your symptom data instead of a separate notebook. Treatment effectiveness is calculated from your logged severity before and after, not a self-rating you have to reconstruct later.

Food and GI symptoms, side by side

Bowel symptoms and endo belly are part of a lot of people's experience with endometriosis, so nutrition logging sits right next to diarrhea, constipation, and stomach pain in your timeline instead of living in a separate food app.

How it fits together

Other apps track each thing in its own silo: pain in one place, cycle in another, sleep somewhere else entirely. Go Go Gaia keeps your cycle, symptoms, nutrition, sleep, and wearable data in one place, then surfaces real patterns from your own data, like a lifestyle factor that lines up with flare days. That connective layer is the same correlation engine behind every condition the app tracks, not a separate tool bolted on for endo.
