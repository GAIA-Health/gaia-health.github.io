Your labs came back normal. So why do you still feel like this?

Veya is the women's health tracker that investigates. It brings your cycle, sleep, food, symptoms, labs and wearable data together, notices the patterns you can't see, and helps you test what's really driving how you feel.

Then you walk into your next appointment with more than a feeling.

HOW IT WORKS
- Gather: say how you feel in one sentence and Veya logs it. Apple Watch and Oura fill in sleep, heart rate and activity on their own.
- Notice: Veya checks your symptoms against your sleep, cycle phase, food, stress and weather, including effects that show up a day or two later.
- Explain: each pattern comes with a simple explanation of what may be going on in your body.
- Test: turn a hunch into a Health Clue, then run an Experiment with a real baseline. You get an honest verdict, including "not enough to tell yet".
- Share: export your Case File, a PDF with your history, patterns, labs and the questions worth asking, and bring it to your doctor.

Veya shows patterns, never a diagnosis. It helps you prepare for your doctor, never replaces one.

BUILT FOR
- Symptoms nobody has explained: fatigue, poor sleep, bloating, mood that swings with your cycle, pain, migraines
- PCOS: irregular and missing cycles, labs and glucose beside your symptoms
- Perimenopause: hot flashes, sleep and mood as your cycle shifts
- PMDD: daily ratings across cycles, a record you can bring to your doctor
- Endometriosis: pain by type and cycle phase, with gut symptoms and food alongside
- Postpartum: tired, foggy or losing hair months after birth, with your sleep and returning cycle side by side
- Also: thyroid, fibroids, adenomyosis, IBD and autoimmune conditions

ONE RECORD FOR EVERY STAGE
Your history doesn't reset when life does. Trying to conceive, IVF, egg freezing, pregnancy and postpartum all live on the same record as your cycles and symptoms, so you never start from zero.
