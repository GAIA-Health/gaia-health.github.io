Trainerize: What It Does Well

Trainerize, now operating as ABC Trainerize under ABC Fitness, is one of the most established names in personal training software, and for good reason. It covers the full loop of remote coaching: an AI-assisted workout builder, meal and macro tracking, habit coaching, in-app messaging, live and on-demand video coaching, a custom-branded mobile app, and Stripe-based payment processing for selling programs or packages.

It scales from independent trainers up to gyms and enterprise health clubs, with tiered plans built around client count. A permanent free Basic plan covers one client, and a 30-day free trial with unlimited clients and no credit card requirement lets you test the full platform before committing. That range, from a single-client hobby coach up to a 500-plus-client studio operation, is genuinely hard to match, and it's the reason Trainerize shows up on nearly every "best coaching software" list.

Where it makes sense to look elsewhere is less about quality and more about fit. Some coaches want a simpler plan structure without the tiered client-count math. Some want white-label branding without paying for a higher tier to get it. Some coaches training female clients want to see health context Trainerize was never built to show, like where a client is in her cycle or how her sleep tracked against a hard training week. None of that is a knock on Trainerize. It just means the four options below are worth a look depending on what you're missing.

Why Coaches Look for Alternatives

Most coaches who search for alternatives to Trainerize aren't unhappy with it. They're checking whether something fits their specific practice better, whether that's price, simplicity, or a kind of client data Trainerize doesn't track.

  • You want a simpler pricing ladder. Trainerize's tiers scale with client count in a way that can feel like a lot of plan-comparing for a small roster. A couple of the alternatives below have flatter, more predictable pricing.
  • You want white-label branding sooner. A custom-branded app is a strong retention tool, and it sits at a different price point depending on the platform.
  • You want more business and CRM tooling built in. Assessments, automated onboarding, and sales workflows matter more to some coaching practices than others.
  • You coach clients where cycle, sleep, or lab context would change your programming. None of the mainstream coaching platforms, Trainerize included, track a client's menstrual cycle or connect it to her training data.

None of that makes Trainerize the wrong choice. It just means the four alternatives below are worth a look if any of that sounds like your practice.

The Alternatives Compared

Here are three established program-delivery platforms plus one app that covers a different gap entirely, in no particular order of quality. For a direct head-to-head on two of the biggest names, see our TrueCoach vs Trainerize comparison.

For a Simpler, Coach-First Setup: TrueCoach

Best if you want: A platform built more around independent coaches and smaller practices, with a workout builder and messaging that feel less enterprise-configured than Trainerize's.

Key Features

  • Drag-and-drop workout builder with a large exercise video library and MyFitnessPal-linked nutrition tracking
  • Real-time in-app messaging and custom habit tracking, assigned daily, weekly, or monthly
  • Wearable data sync with Apple Health, Garmin, and WHOOP on Standard and Pro plans, plus Stripe-based payments

Strengths

  • Markets specifically to independent fitness coaches and healthcare-adjacent professionals like physical therapists, not just gyms
  • 14-day free trial with full platform access and no credit card required, as of August 2026

Limitations

  • No free permanent plan the way Trainerize has one for a single client, and plans are priced per client tier (5, 20, or 50 clients) rather than per additional client
  • No menstrual cycle or hormone context for the coach

Pricing (as of August 2026): Starter around $26/month for 5 clients, Standard around $58/month for 20 clients, Pro around $137/month for 50 clients, cheaper with annual billing.

Learn more: truecoach.co

For White-Label Branding at a Lower Price Point: Everfit

Best if you want: A custom-branded coaching app at a lower price point than Trainerize charges for the same feature, plus per-client pricing that drops as your roster grows.

Key Features

  • Workout programming with a library of over 1,000 exercises, plus nutrition tracking, meal plans, and macro monitoring
  • Habit and task tracking with automated reminders, custom forms, and questionnaires
  • Custom app branding (logo and colors) and team collaboration tools, included on paid plans

Strengths

  • Free forever Starter plan for up to 5 clients, more room than Trainerize's single-client free tier
  • Per-client cost drops as your roster grows, from roughly $3 per client at 5 clients down to under $1 per client at 300, as of August 2026

Limitations

  • Full white-label branding and team features sit on higher-priced Studio and Enterprise tiers, similar to how Trainerize gates its own branding
  • No menstrual cycle or hormone context for the coach, and smaller name recognition than Trainerize or TrueCoach

Pricing (as of August 2026): Starter free for up to 5 clients. Pro roughly $16 to $255/month scaling with client count. Studio from around $88/month for 50-plus clients. Enterprise custom-priced.

Learn more: everfit.io

For Deeper Business and CRM Tools: PT Distinction

Best if you want: A platform that leans further into running the business side of coaching, not just delivering programs, with built-in assessments and automated client workflows.

Key Features

  • Training program builder plus an AI program generator, built-in and custom client assessment tools
  • Nutrition coaching tools with an AI meal planner, habit coaching, and automated email reminders
  • Group functionality for challenges, plus custom-branded apps and payment processing for package sales

Strengths

  • Built-in assessment tools and automated workflows go further into practice management than a pure workout-delivery tool
  • 1-month free trial, cancel anytime, no hidden fees per the company

Limitations

  • Custom app branding is reserved for higher tiers on some plan structures, the same trade-off as Trainerize and Everfit, and there's no menstrual cycle or hormone context for the coach

Pricing (as of August 2026): Basic $19.90/month for up to 3 clients, plus $6 per additional client. Pro $59.90/month for up to 25 clients, plus $2.40 per additional client. Master $89.90/month for up to 50 clients, plus $1.60 per additional client. All tiers come with a 1-month free trial.

Learn more: ptdistinction.com

How to Choose, by Coach Type

The right pick depends less on which platform is "best" and more on what kind of coaching business you're running and what you already have covered.

  • Solo trainer just starting to take clients online: Trainerize's free Basic plan or TrueCoach's trial are the lowest-friction ways to see if a coaching app fits your workflow before you commit to a paid tier.
  • Growing practice that outgrew spreadsheets and DMs: Everfit's scaling per-client pricing or TrueCoach's tiered plans both work well once you're managing more than a handful of clients but aren't ready for gym-scale software.
  • Established coaching business that wants sales and CRM tools built in: PT Distinction's assessment and automation features are built for exactly this, and Trainerize's higher tiers cover similar ground at gym scale.
  • Coach whose clients are mostly women and whose programming gap is health context, not program delivery: Keep whatever platform you're already using for workouts and payments, and add the Go Go Gaia coach portal alongside it for the cycle, sleep, and lab picture. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best alternative to Trainerize?

There isn't one best alternative, since the apps below are built for different-sized coaching businesses. TrueCoach fits solo trainers who want a simpler, coach-first workflow. Everfit fits coaches who want white-label branding at a lower price point than Trainerize charges for the same. PT Distinction leans further into business and CRM tools for trainers running a full coaching practice. Go Go Gaia for Professionals fits coaches whose gap isn't program delivery but seeing a client's cycle, sleep, and lab data alongside training.

Is Trainerize free?

Trainerize has a permanent free Basic plan for one client, plus a 30-day free trial with unlimited clients and no credit card required, as of August 2026. Paid plans start around $9 a month for a small client roster and scale up from there based on how many clients you're training.

What's the difference between Trainerize and TrueCoach?

Both are established coaching platforms with workout builders, messaging, and habit tracking, but they lean different directions. Trainerize leans toward gyms and studios with tiered plans that scale into the hundreds of clients and add-ons like live video coaching. TrueCoach markets itself more toward independent coaches and smaller practices, including physical therapists and other healthcare-adjacent professionals, with a simpler plan structure. We cover this comparison in more depth in our dedicated TrueCoach vs Trainerize guide.

Do any Trainerize alternatives track a client's menstrual cycle?

Not the program-delivery platforms in this comparison. Trainerize, TrueCoach, Everfit, and PT Distinction are all built around workout programming and client communication, with no menstrual cycle tracking for the coach to see. Go Go Gaia for Professionals is built specifically to show cycle phase alongside sleep, training compliance, and labs, but it isn't a program-delivery platform in the way the others are, so some coaches use it alongside one of them rather than instead of it.

Can I switch from Trainerize without losing my client data?

Most of these platforms let you export workout history and client lists in some form, but there's no universal one-click migration between coaching apps. Check each platform's current export and onboarding documentation before you commit, and plan on a short overlap period where you're re-entering program templates in the new tool while clients finish out their current block in Trainerize.

Final Thoughts

Trainerize earns its reputation. It covers program delivery end to end, scales from a single client up to a full gym roster, and its free tier and trial make it easy to test before paying anything. For most coaches already using it well, there's no reason to switch.

If you're looking around anyway, match the alternative to the actual gap. Want something simpler and coach-first? TrueCoach. Want branding without the higher-tier price tag? Everfit. Want assessments and business automation built in? PT Distinction.
