Balance vs Caria vs Flo for Perimenopause (2026)

Balance suits you if you want a menopause-specific app with clinician-led content, Caria if you want structured programs, and Flo if you want to stay in a cycle tracker. Compared on listings and pricing as of October 2026.

By Veya Team • Published October 16, 2026 • Last updated: October 2026 • 8 min read • App Comparison

Full Transparency

This comparison is published by Holland Neurotech Inc., the company behind Veya. Veya appears as one column among equals, with its gaps listed. If Balance, Caria or Flo fits you better, use it.

Quick Answer

  • Menopause-specific app with clinician-led content: Balance. Founded by Dr Louise Newson, with a free core and balance+ premium.[1]
  • Structured CBT-style programs: Caria. Its listing describes programs, quick-relief audio and community.[2]
  • Staying in a cycle tracker you already use: Flo. Its listing describes a Perimenopause Score and a Menopause Timeline.[3]
  • Cycle continuity through irregular cycles, alongside sleep and nutrition: Veya, which is not menopause-first.
This article is educational and not medical advice. It describes what each app lets you log. It does not recommend any treatment.

What is the difference between Balance, Caria and Flo?

Balance is built for menopause, with a free core and an expert-led premium tier. Caria is built around guided programs for symptoms. Flo is a cycle tracker that added a Perimenopause Score and Menopause Timeline. All three are free to download with in-app purchases.[1][2][3]

Which app tracks perimenopause symptoms best?

They track differently. Balance lists symptom and period tracking with a Health Report for appointments. Caria lists symptom and activity logging with Siri Shortcuts and a daily symptom score. Flo lists over 70 trackable symptoms and moods. Veya logs perimenopause symptoms next to sleep, nutrition and wearable data.[1][2][3]

Which suits you depends on whether you want a menopause-first log, a program, or one tracker for your whole cycle. The best perimenopause tracking app roundup compares seven apps, and do period apps work during perimenopause explains why predictions wobble when cycles change.

Can these apps log HRT or treatment?

None of the three listings describes treatment logging. Balance's listing mentions symptom tracking and a Health Report, Caria's refers to treatment recommendations shared by other users, and Flo's lists symptom and well-being tracking.[1][2][3] We cannot confirm in-app medication fields from the listings alone.

Veya's roundup entry lists medication and supplement tracking, including HRT. That describes a logging field, not guidance on what to take, and no app here recommends treatment.

Which has community and clinician content?

Balance and Caria both list community features, and Balance's balance+ tier adds live sessions with Dr Newson and guest experts. Caria cites health and wellness experts without naming clinicians. Flo's Premium lists expert videos and Secret Chats.[1][2][3]

Caria's app has a published trial: a 6-week randomized study of 149 women reported lower hot flash severity than a web-education group, though outcomes were self-reported and short.[4] For symptoms that overlap with perimenopause, see our guides on tracking sleep across your cycle, brain fog and joint pain.

What does each app cost in 2026?

Balance lists balance+ at $49.99 or $98.99 per year and $3.99, $4.99 or $10.99 per month, without labeling which tier is which. Caria lists $5.99 per month and $47.99 per year, though its description text quotes $9.99 and $49.99. Flo lists Premium purchases up to $59.99.[1][2][3]

These are App Store in-app purchase lists as of October 2026 and vary by region and offer. Flo's listing also shows $47.99 and $14.99 tiers, and says core tracking is free. Check the price inside the app before you subscribe.

What do the privacy labels show?

All three carry developer-reported privacy labels that Apple has not verified. Balance lists health data as used for analytics and contact info for advertising or marketing. Caria lists health, purchase and contact data as linked to you, used for analytics and personalization.[1][2]

Flo settled with the FTC in 2021 over sharing health data, covered in Flo vs Clue vs Glow, and offers an Anonymous Mode.[5] Read each privacy policy before logging symptoms, especially anything related to treatment.

Where does Veya fit?

Veya keeps tracking through irregular and skipped cycles and links symptoms to sleep, diet and activity, with wearable data from Apple Watch, Oura Ring and Garmin. Core tracking is free, and Pro is $9.99 per month or $49.99 per year as of October 2026.

It is not menopause-first. There is no community, no clinician-led content and no CBT programs, and it is iOS only with a smaller perimenopause user base than the three above. The perimenopause guide and menopause guide explain the stages without needing any app.

Which should you choose: Balance, Caria or Flo?

Choose Balance if:

  • You want a menopause-specific app with a free core
  • You want clinician-led sessions and a Health Report for appointments

Choose Caria if:

  • You want structured programs and quick-relief audio
  • You are open to a paid tier for the full programs

Choose Flo if:

  • You already use Flo and want perimenopause tools in the same place
  • You are comfortable with its privacy history or Anonymous Mode

Choose Veya if:

  • Your cycle has become irregular and you want tracking to keep going
  • You want symptoms next to sleep, nutrition and wearable data

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Balance app?

Balance is a menopause app from Balance App Limited, founded by Dr Louise Newson. Its listing describes a free core with articles, symptom and period tracking, community and a Health Report, with balance+ as a paid tier.

Is Caria free?

Caria is free to download with in-app purchases. Its listing shows Premium at $5.99 per month or $47.99 per year, while the description text quotes $9.99 and $49.99, so check inside the app.

Does Flo have a perimenopause mode?

Flo's listing describes a Perimenopause Score to follow symptom changes and a Menopause Timeline. It does not say which perimenopause features need Premium.

Which app logs hormone therapy?

None of the three listings describes treatment logging. Veya's roundup entry lists medication tracking including HRT. Apps only record what you enter and do not recommend treatment.

Which has the best community?

Balance and Caria both list community features. Flo's community-style Secret Chats are listed under Premium.

Do I need a menopause app if my periods are still happening?

Perimenopause usually overlaps with periods, so a cycle tracker, a menopause app or both can fit. See do period apps work during perimenopause for how predictions behave.
