Flo vs Clue vs Glow: Free Period Trackers Compared (2026)

Flo suits you if you want the broadest toolset and an anonymous community, Clue if your cycles are irregular and you want flexible logging, and Glow if you are trying to conceive. They differ most in what costs money and in their privacy records, both checked against each app's own listing and public sources as of October 2026.

By Veya Team • Published September 7, 2026 • Last updated: October 2026 • 9 min read • App Comparison

Quick Answer

Flo, Clue, and Glow all started as period trackers and grew in different directions. Here's the fast version:

  • General period tracking, broadest toolset: Flo. Core cycle tracking is free; per its App Store listing, Secret Chats, the health assistant and in-depth pregnancy features are Premium.
  • Irregular cycles, possible PCOS: Clue. Its App Store listing says you can track symptoms even without regular bleeding.
  • Trying to conceive: Glow. Its site describes an Ovulation Tracker for people trying to conceive, plus separate Pregnancy and Baby trackers, and its September 2026 update added free daily voice check-ins with an AI companion, Glow Coach.[8]
This article is educational and not medical advice. None of the apps below are birth control, and none of them diagnose PCOS or any other condition. A pattern you notice while tracking is something to bring to a doctor, not a self-diagnosis.

Flo, Clue, and Glow all started as period trackers and still come up in "which app should I download" searches. Flo layers fertility, pregnancy and community features onto general cycle tracking. Clue describes a science-first approach with published research collaborations. Glow splits its tools across separate apps, with an Ovulation Tracker aimed at people trying to conceive. The differences that matter most aren't about which has more features. It's what costs money, and which app's design fits what you're tracking for.

Which is better: Flo, Clue, or Glow?

It depends on what you track for. What Do You Get for Free in Flo, Clue, and Glow?

All three are free to download, but each keeps its deeper tools behind a subscription: Flo's Premium covers the Health Assistant, Secret Chats and a Symptom Checker, Clue Plus covers deeper analysis and advanced predictions, and Glow Premium covers an advanced cycle chart, cycle analysis and a forecast, per each App Store listing as of October 2026.[1][2][8] Here is what that means app by app.

Flo's free tier

Per Flo's App Store listing, the app is free to download with core cycle-tracking tools, while Secret Chats, the 24/7 Health Assistant and in-depth pregnancy features are listed as Premium (as of October 2026).[1]

Clue's free tier

Per Clue's App Store listing, Clue is free to download with in-app purchases, and Clue Plus adds deeper analysis, advanced predictions and personalized insights.[2] Some wearable syncing may also need Clue Plus, so check the app for your tier.

Glow's free tier

Glow's site lists four separate apps: an Ovulation Tracker, a Pregnancy Tracker, a Baby Tracker and a Period Tracker (Eve). Glow Premium adds the GlowGPT assistant, advanced analytics, premium articles and ad-free use.[3] The App Store listing says the app is free to download, that Premium unlocks the Advanced Cycle Chart, Cycle Analysis, Forecast and Advanced BBT chart, and that Glow Coach voice check-ins are free.[8]

Put plainly: Flo and Clue both keep deeper features behind a subscription, and Glow splits its tools across four apps while listing AI, analytics and ad-free use under Premium.

What changed in Flo, Clue, and Glow in 2026?

Flo's listing now promotes a Perimenopause Score and Timeline with a Premium Symptom Checker, Glow shipped free Glow Coach voice check-ins in September 2026, and Clue now ships updates weekly (version 298.0 as of October 2026). Each fact below comes from the app's own listing or a named report.[1][2][8]

  • Flo: The listing names a Symptom Checker (not available in the UK, EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland), TTC fertility insights and the Health Assistant as Premium, and promotes perimenopause Score and Timeline features.[1] Flo also published, with Mayo Clinic, a perimenopause-awareness study of more than 17,000 users in 2026.[9] The listing is at version 10.8.0 (October 2026) and is described as a performance update.[1]
  • Clue: The listing describes Clue Plus as deeper analysis, advanced predictions and personalized insights, and the app releases a new version weekly.[2]
  • Glow: Versions 12.1.0 and 12.1.1 (September 2026) added daily voice check-ins with Glow Coach, an AI wellness companion that gives a Wellness Score. Earlier releases added a Cyclescope experience and a Personal Get Pregnant Plan.[8]

What do you get with Flo?

Flo is a general cycle-health app first, with fertility and pregnancy tracking layered in. Its App Store listing claims over 570 million downloads worldwide (as of July 2026).[1]

Features

  • Cycle tracking with AI-powered predictions
  • AI Health Assistant that checks in on symptoms like cramps and fatigue (Premium)
  • Secret Chats: anonymous community discussions (Premium)
  • Pregnancy mode for after conception; in-depth pregnancy features are Premium

Strengths

Community and pregnancy tools. Per its listing, Secret Chats and in-depth pregnancy features are Premium, and the app describes an Anonymous Mode privacy option.[1]

Limitations

Several features, including the AI Health Assistant, sit behind Flo Premium, and its privacy history (below) is worth weighing.

Pricing

Free: Core cycle-tracking tools. Premium adds Secret Chats, the Health Assistant and in-depth pregnancy features.

Flo Premium: App Store purchases are listed up to $59.99 (as of October 2026), with lower-priced tiers also listed.[1]

Download: Available on iOS and Android

What do you get with Clue?

Clue was founded in Berlin in 2012 and lists research collaborations on its About page. Its App Store listing says you can track symptoms even without regular bleeding.[2]

Features

  • Cycle and symptom tracking with prediction algorithms informed by published research
  • Separate tracking modes for Period Tracking, Conceive, Pregnancy, and Perimenopause[2]
  • Wearable sync with Apple Watch, Oura, WHOOP, Withings and Fitbit (some syncing may require Clue Plus)
  • A minimal interface

Strengths

Built to log irregular cycles. Clue's listing says you can track symptoms even without regular bleeding, and it has separate modes for periods, trying to conceive, pregnancy and perimenopause.[2]

Limitations

Deeper analysis and advanced predictions sit behind Clue Plus.[2]

Pricing

Free: Basic cycle and symptom tracking.

Clue Plus: $39.99/year or $9.99/month on the App Store (as of October 2026).[2]

Download: Available on iOS and Android

What do you get with Glow?

Glow's site describes four apps: an Ovulation Tracker for people trying to conceive or wanting to understand their cycle, a Pregnancy Tracker, a Baby Tracker, and a Period Tracker (Eve).[3]

Features

  • Ovulation Tracker for cycle and ovulation tracking, including for trying to conceive
  • Pregnancy Tracker with due-date calculation and a baby registry
  • Baby Tracker for sleep, diapers, and feeding
  • GlowGPT health assistant (Premium)

Strengths

Built around trying to conceive. Glow's site describes its Ovulation Tracker for people trying to conceive, with separate Pregnancy and Baby trackers for later stages.[3]

Limitations

Glow has two documented privacy incidents (below): a 2020 California settlement over a consent flaw, and a 2024 bug that exposed profile data. GlowGPT, analytics and ad-free use cost extra with Premium.

Pricing

Free: Glow's page does not spell out which features are free, so check each app.

Glow Premium: $59.99/year after a 7-day free trial (as of October 2026).[3]

Download: Available on iOS and Android

Which should you choose: Flo, Clue, or Glow?

Choose by situation, not by feature count. Flo fits general tracking with community, Clue fits irregular cycles and flexible logging (its listing says you can track symptoms without regular bleeding), and Glow fits trying to conceive, with separate Pregnancy and Baby trackers for later. All three are free to download, and none is birth control.[1][2][3]

Choose Flo if you're tracking general period health:

  • You want a broad toolset and an anonymous community (Secret Chats, Premium)
  • You want a general tracker with a pregnancy mode for later
  • You're comfortable with Flo's privacy history, or willing to use Anonymous Mode

Choose Clue if your cycles are irregular or PCOS is part of the picture:

  • You want to track symptoms even without regular bleeding
  • Privacy and a science-first build matter to you
  • You plan to bring what you track to a doctor, not rely on the app to diagnose anything

Choose Glow if trying to conceive is why you're tracking:

  • You want a tracker aimed at trying to conceive
  • You like separate Pregnancy and Baby trackers for later stages
  • You've read Glow's current privacy policy and are comfortable with its history

If you actually want your cycle tracked alongside mood, sleep, fitness, and nutrition, that's a different scope than any of the three above. What Does Each App's Privacy Record Show?

Flo has the longest enforcement history (an FTC order finalized June 2021 and a $59.5 million class settlement with a final hearing October 29, 2026), Glow has two documented incidents (a 2020 California settlement and a 2024 bug), and we found no regulator action for Clue.[4][5] The record, with dates:

Privacy dimension Flo Clue Glow
Company base US-based Berlin, Germany (EU GDPR) US-based
Regulator / legal action FTC order 2021 (final June 2021)[4][7] None that we found CA AG settlement, 2020[5]
Data-exposure incident Health data shared with Facebook, Google and others, per the FTC None that we found API bug, ~25M profiles (not health logs), Feb 2024[6]
Anonymized mode Anonymous Mode described on listing No No

Flo: The FTC announced a proposed settlement with Flo Health on January 13, 2021, alleging it shared health data from millions of users with third parties including Facebook's and Google's analytics divisions, AppsFlyer and Flurry despite privacy promises; the final order was announced June 22, 2021.[4][7] A related $59.5 million class settlement across Google, Flurry and Flo has a court final approval hearing on October 29, 2026, and a jury returned a verdict for the plaintiffs against Meta on August 1, 2025.[11] Flo's listing describes an Anonymous Mode.[1]

Clue: Berlin-based; its privacy policy says its data handling meets German and EU legal requirements and that sensitive health data is never shared with or sold to advertisers.[10] We found no FTC order or state settlement for Clue in our checks.

Glow: California's Attorney General reached a settlement with Glow, Inc. in September 2020 with a $250,000 civil penalty, alleging that Partner Connect linked accounts without authorization from the user whose data would be shared and that password-change requests were not adequately verified.[5] A TechCrunch report of February 13, 2024 said a bug exposed names, age group, location, user IDs and profile images for around 25 million users, which the researcher said was fixed about a week after reporting.[6]

Flo has the longest enforcement history, Glow has two incidents four years apart, and we found none for Clue. None of that makes any one app unsafe, but it's worth knowing.

Frequently asked questions about Flo, Clue, and Glow

Which is the best free period tracker: Flo, Clue, or Glow?

Flo and Clue both keep deeper features behind a subscription (Flo: Secret Chats, Health Assistant, in-depth pregnancy features; Clue: Clue Plus analysis and predictions). Glow splits its tools across four apps and lists GlowGPT, analytics and ad-free use under Premium.

Is Clue or Flo better for irregular cycles or PCOS?

Clue's App Store listing says you can track symptoms even without regular bleeding, which is why it is a common pick for irregular cycles. Neither app diagnoses PCOS. Bringing what you track to a doctor is the useful next step.

Is Glow good for trying to conceive?

Glow's site describes an Ovulation Tracker for people trying to conceive, plus Pregnancy and Baby trackers for later. Flo and Clue also offer fertility tracking (Clue lists a Conceive mode), but Glow's tools are organized around TTC.

Which app has the strongest privacy record: Flo, Clue, or Glow?

Flo settled with the FTC in 2021, and we found no regulator action for Clue. Glow paid $250,000 to California's AG in 2020, and a 2024 bug exposed profile (not health) data for around 25 million users.

Do Flo, Clue, and Glow have an AI assistant or coach?

Flo lists a 24/7 virtual Health Assistant as a Premium feature, and Glow offers GlowGPT in Premium plus free daily voice check-ins with Glow Coach since September 2026. Clue's listing describes personalized insights in Clue Plus rather than a chat assistant. These are App Store listing descriptions as of October 2026, not tests of how well each works.
