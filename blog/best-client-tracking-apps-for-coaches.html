Best Client Tracking Apps for Coaches and Personal Trainers (2026)

Client tracking apps let coaches and personal trainers build workout programs, message clients, log check-ins, and see progress in one shared dashboard instead of a spreadsheet and a text thread. Some focus on program delivery and business tools. One newer entrant adds a client's own health context, cycle, sleep, and habit data, with her consent. Here's how five of them compare.

By Go Go Gaia Team • Published August 25, 2026 • 10 min read • For Coaches

Educational content for fitness and wellness professionals, not medical or business advice. Pricing and features were verified against each company's website in August 2026 and can change.

Quick Answer

There's no single best client tracking app. It depends on what your coaching business actually needs. TrueCoach is best for straightforward program delivery, Trainerize for scaling from one client to a full studio, Everfit for AI-assisted programming and forms-based check-ins, PT Distinction for a branded app included at a flat price, and Go Go Gaia for Professionals for a whole-person view of cycle, sleep, and habit data your clients already track. Here's how all five compare:

  • Best for straightforward program delivery: TrueCoach. Fast workout builder, large exercise video library, wearable sync
  • Best for scaling a coaching business: Trainerize. Free single-client tier up through studio and gym plans
  • Best for AI-assisted programming: Everfit. AI workout builder, forms and questionnaires, community forums
  • Best for a branded app at a flat price: PT Distinction. AI programming and meal planning included on every tier
  • Best for whole-person client data: Go Go Gaia for Professionals. Cycle, sleep, and habit logs in one consented view, free for up to 3 clients

How We Picked These Apps

We shortlisted five client tracking platforms that coaches and personal trainers actually use, from dedicated program-delivery software to a newer whole-person health data portal, and compared them on the criteria that matter most for running a coaching business: workout programming, client check-ins and messaging, nutrition and habit tracking, and how much of a client's health context, if any, is visible beyond what she reports manually. Pricing and features were re-verified directly on each company's website in August 2026.

Full disclosure: this site is published by Holland Neurotech Inc., the maker of Go Go Gaia. The list isn't ranked by "best overall." Each app is presented for the coaching need it fits, we include our own product only where it genuinely belongs, and we say plainly where a competitor is the stronger choice.

Most coaches start with a spreadsheet and a group chat. It works fine until you're managing more than four or five clients, and then check-ins get missed, programs get overwritten, and you're scrolling back through texts trying to remember who reported what.

Client tracking software fixes the logistics: one place to build a program, message a client, and see what she logged. Where these platforms actually differ is in the client information they surface: program templates and exercise libraries in four of them, and, in one newer entrant, a client's own health data (cycle phase, sleep, habit logs) if she chooses to share it. This guide compares five platforms on programming, check-ins, pricing, and what health context is visible, if any.

The 5 Apps Compared

For Straightforward Program Delivery: TrueCoach

Best if you want: A fast, no-frills way to build and assign workouts without configuring a lot of extra business tooling.

Key Features

  • Drag-and-drop workout builder with templates you can copy and reuse in under a minute
  • In-app messaging with photo, video, and GIF support
  • 3,500+ professionally recorded exercise demonstration videos
  • MyFitnessPal integration for macro and calorie tracking
  • Custom daily, weekly, or monthly habit assignments
  • Wearable sync on Standard and Pro plans (Apple Health, Garmin, Oura, WHOOP)
  • Stripe-powered recurring billing

Strengths

  • Built for programming to feel fast, not for running your whole business inside the app
  • Large, professionally produced exercise video library
  • Wearable sync is included on paid plans rather than sold as a separate add-on
  • Used across strength and conditioning, bodybuilding, CrossFit, and physical therapy settings

Limitations

  • No permanent free tier, only a 14-day trial
  • Cost climbs with client count: $136.99/month once you're managing 50 clients
  • No cycle, sleep, or lab data

Who Should Choose This

TrueCoach is a good fit if you coach strength and conditioning, bodybuilding, or CrossFit clients and want programming and messaging to just work without a lot of setup. See our TrueCoach vs Trainerize comparison for a closer look at how the two handle programming differently.

Pricing (as of August 2026): Starter $26.34/month (up to 5 clients), Standard $57.99/month (up to 20 clients), Pro $136.99/month (up to 50 clients). 14-day free trial, no card required.

Learn more: truecoach.co

For Scaling a Coaching Business: Trainerize

Best if you want: A platform that grows with you, from a single free client up through a full studio or gym roster.

Key Features

  • Custom client programs plus pre-built fitness content
  • In-app messaging with voice messages and auto-responses
  • Meal and macro tracking, with optional advanced nutrition tools
  • Custom and pre-made habit tracking with streaks and badges
  • Live and on-demand video coaching (add-on)
  • Wearable integrations with Garmin, Withings, Fitbit, and MyFitnessPal
  • Scheduling, Stripe payments, and product sales

Strengths

  • A genuinely free Basic tier for a single client, not just a trial
  • 30-day free trial with unlimited clients before you commit to a paid tier
  • Full set of business tools: scheduling, payments, and product sales alongside programming
  • Pricing ladder that scales cleanly from a solo trainer up to a 500+ client studio

Limitations

  • Video coaching is a separate add-on, not included by default
  • Pricing tiers get more layered as your roster grows
  • No cycle, sleep, or lab data

Who Should Choose This

Trainerize fits coaches and studios planning to grow past a handful of clients who want scheduling and payments handled in the same place as programming. If TrueCoach doesn't have the business tools you need, our Trainerize alternatives roundup covers more options in this same category.

Pricing (as of August 2026): Basic free (1 client), Grow $9-10/month (2 clients), Pro 5 from $23/month (5-200 clients), Studio Plus from $248/month (500+ clients). 30-day free trial, no card required.

Learn more: trainerize.com

For AI-Assisted Programming: Everfit

Best if you want: AI help building workouts and meal plans, plus forms-based check-ins for a larger roster.

Key Features

  • Custom workout builder with 1,000+ exercises and an AI Workout Builder
  • Direct messaging and inbox for client communication
  • Forms and questionnaires for structured check-ins
  • Community forums for group engagement
  • Food journal and macro tracking, with a meal plans and recipe books add-on
  • Habit tracking with automated task reminders
  • Custom branding and team management tools

Strengths

  • Free Starter tier covers up to 5 clients with core features
  • Forms and questionnaires make structured, repeatable check-ins easy to set up
  • Community forums support group and cohort-style coaching
  • Scales to enterprise fitness brands with custom pricing and team management

Limitations

  • Some tools, like AI meal planning, sit behind add-ons rather than being included outright
  • Pricing spans a wide range ($16-255/month on Pro) depending on client count
  • No cycle, sleep, or lab data

Who Should Choose This

Everfit is worth a look if you want AI to speed up program and meal-plan building, or you're running group coaching and want forms and community features built in.

Pricing (as of August 2026): Starter free (up to 5 clients), Pro $16-255/month scaling with client count, Studio $88-358/month (50+ clients), Enterprise custom pricing. Free trial, no card required.

Learn more: everfit.io

For a Branded App at a Flat Price: PT Distinction

Best if you want: Your own branded coaching app without a separate line-item fee for it, plus AI tools included on every plan.

Key Features

  • AI Program Builder and AI Meal Planner, included on every tier
  • Nutrition coaching, assessments, and group training tools
  • Email, SMS, and in-app messaging with pre-scheduling
  • Habit tracking and automated workflows
  • Custom-branded iOS and Android apps (Pro and Master tiers)

Strengths

  • AI programming and meal-planning tools are included rather than sold as add-ons
  • Branded client app comes with the Pro tier instead of a separate fee
  • Flat, predictable monthly pricing with clearly listed per-extra-client costs
  • 1-month free trial you can cancel without being charged

Limitations

  • No permanently free tier, only the 1-month trial
  • The entry Basic tier caps at 3 clients before per-client fees apply
  • Branded app isn't available on the Basic tier
  • No cycle, sleep, or lab data

Who Should Choose This

PT Distinction fits coaches who want their own branded app from early on and don't want to pay extra for AI programming and meal-planning tools that other platforms sell separately.

Pricing (as of August 2026): Basic $19.90/month (3 clients, +$6/extra client), Pro $59.90/month (25 clients, +$2.40/extra), Master $89.90/month (50 clients, +$1.60/extra). 1-month free trial.

Learn more: ptdistinction.com

Frequently Asked Questions

Do coaching clients need a special app to share health data with a coach?

With Go Go Gaia for Professionals, a client logs her own data in the free Go Go Gaia iOS app and chooses what to share with a coach through a consent-based invite. The other four platforms in this guide don't request cycle, sleep, or lab data at all, since they're built around workout programming and check-ins rather than a client's broader health picture. Our guide on what app lets a coach see a client's cycle and sleep data walks through what that consent step looks like and what a coach actually sees on the other side of it.

What's the difference between fitness coaching software and a health-data client portal?

Coaching software like TrueCoach, Trainerize, Everfit, and PT Distinction is built to deliver a program: build workouts, message clients, track habits, and often handle scheduling and payments. A health-data portal like Go Go Gaia for Professionals is narrower. It's built to surface health context a client already tracks, like cycle phase, sleep, and habit logs, with a lighter program editor rather than a full delivery platform.

Is it worth paying for AI programming or meal-planning tools?

It depends on how much time you currently spend building programs and meal plans from scratch. Everfit and PT Distinction both offer AI program and meal-plan generation, with PT Distinction bundling it into every tier and Everfit's AI meal planning sitting behind an add-on. If you already have templates you reuse, a simpler drag-and-drop builder like TrueCoach's may get you there just as fast without the extra cost.

Can I use more than one of these tools together?

Plenty of coaches do. A common setup is a program-delivery platform like TrueCoach or Trainerize for building and assigning workouts, paired with a lighter tool for anything that platform doesn't cover. Since Go Go Gaia for Professionals is a client portal rather than a full programming suite, it's designed to sit alongside whichever program-delivery software you're already using, not replace it.

How much does client tracking software cost for a small coaching business?

For a coach starting out with a handful of clients, Trainerize and Everfit both offer free tiers (1 and 5 clients respectively), and Go Go Gaia for Professionals is free for up to 3 clients. TrueCoach and PT Distinction don't have permanent free tiers, but both offer trial periods before their paid plans start around $20 to $26 a month. Costs scale up from there as your roster grows, so it's worth mapping your expected client count against each platform's tiers before committing.
