Best Client Tracking Apps for Coaches and Personal Trainers (2026)

No single client tracking app fits every coach, so pick by need: TrueCoach for straightforward program delivery, Trainerize for growing from one client to a studio, Everfit for AI-assisted programming and forms, PT Distinction for a branded app with published per-client pricing, and Veya for Professionals for a client's own consented cycle, sleep, and habit data.

By Veya Team • Published August 25, 2026 • Last updated: October 2026 • 10 min read • For Coaches

Educational content for fitness and wellness professionals, not medical or business advice. Pricing was re-checked against each company's pricing page on October 16, 2026 (features in September 2026) and can change.

Quick Answer

The right app depends on what your coaching business needs. Here's how the five compare by use case:

  • For straightforward program delivery: TrueCoach. Drag-and-drop workout builder, 3,500+ exercise videos, wearable sync
  • For scaling a coaching business: Trainerize. Free single-client tier up through studio plans
  • For AI-assisted programming: Everfit. AI workout builder, forms and questionnaires, community forums
  • For a branded app with published per-client pricing: PT Distinction. AI program and meal-planning tools that PT Distinction lists as included
  • For whole-person client data: Veya for Professionals. Cycle, sleep, and habit logs in one consented view, free for up to 3 clients

How We Picked These Apps

We shortlisted five client tracking platforms that coaches and personal trainers actually use, from dedicated program-delivery software to a newer whole-person health data portal, and compared them on the criteria that matter most for running a coaching business: workout programming, client check-ins and messaging, nutrition and habit tracking, and how much of a client's health context, if any, is visible beyond what she reports manually. Pricing was re-checked on each company's pricing page on October 16, 2026, features were last verified on each company's site in September 2026, and we read each client app's App Store listing (rating, version date) the same day.

Full disclosure: this site is published by Holland Neurotech Inc., the maker of Veya. The list isn't ranked by "best overall." Each app is presented for the coaching need it fits, we include our own product only where it genuinely belongs, and we say plainly where a competitor is the stronger choice.

Most coaches start with a spreadsheet and a group chat. It tends to work until your roster grows, and then check-ins get missed, programs get overwritten, and you're scrolling back through texts trying to remember who reported what.

Client tracking software fixes the logistics: one place to build a program, message a client, and see what she logged. Where these platforms actually differ is in the client information they surface: program templates and exercise libraries in four of them, and, in one newer entrant, a client's own health data (cycle phase, sleep, habit logs) if she chooses to share it. This guide compares five platforms on programming, check-ins, pricing, and what health context is visible, if any.

For Straightforward Program Delivery: TrueCoach

Best if you want: A fast, no-frills way to build and assign workouts without configuring a lot of extra business tooling. TrueCoach describes itself as software for personal trainers, as of September 2026.[1]

Key Features

  • Drag-and-drop workout builder with reusable templates
  • In-app and group messaging with multimedia support
  • 3,500+ professionally recorded exercise demonstration videos, as of September 2026[1]
  • MyFitnessPal integration for macro and calorie tracking
  • Custom daily, weekly, or monthly habit assignments
  • Wearable sync with Apple Health, Garmin, Oura, and WHOOP (confirm which plans include it), as of September 2026[1]
  • Stripe-powered recurring billing

Strengths

  • Built for programming to feel fast, not for running your whole business inside the app
  • Large, professionally produced exercise video library
  • Named wearable integrations include Apple Health, Garmin, Oura, and WHOOP
  • Clients use the app at no cost to them, per TrueCoach's site

Limitations

  • No permanent free tier, only a 14-day trial
  • Cost climbs with client count: $136.99/month listed for up to 50 clients
  • No cycle or lab data found on its official pages as of September 2026

Who Should Choose This

TrueCoach is a good fit if you coach strength and conditioning, bodybuilding, or CrossFit clients and want programming and messaging to just work without a lot of setup. See our TrueCoach vs Trainerize comparison for a closer look at how the two handle programming differently, or our roundup of TrueCoach alternatives if it's not quite the right fit.

Pricing (as of October 16, 2026): Starter $26.34/month (up to 5 clients), Standard $57.99/month (up to 20 clients), Pro $136.99/month (up to 50 clients). 14-day free trial, no card required. Check TrueCoach's pricing page for monthly versus annual billing.

Client app: TrueCoach on the App Store is free for clients and rated 4.86/5 from about 35.7K ratings, with a version released October 14, 2026.

Learn more: truecoach.co

For Scaling a Coaching Business: Trainerize

Best if you want: A platform that grows with you, from a single free client up through a full studio or gym roster. Trainerize (now branded ABC Trainerize) cites more than 400,000 trainers and coaches on the platform, as of September 2026.[2]

Key Features

  • Custom client programs plus pre-built fitness content
  • In-app messaging with voice messages and auto-responses
  • Meal and macro tracking
  • Custom and pre-made habit tracking with streaks and badges
  • Video coaching tools (check which plans or add-ons include them)
  • Wearable integrations with Apple Watch, Garmin, Withings, Fitbit, and MyFitnessPal, as of September 2026[2]
  • Scheduling, Stripe payments, and product sales

Strengths

  • A genuinely free Basic tier for a single client, not just a trial
  • 30-day free trial with unlimited clients before you commit to a paid tier
  • Full set of business tools: scheduling, payments, and product sales alongside programming
  • Pricing ladder that scales cleanly from a solo trainer up to a 500+ client studio

Limitations

  • Some features, like video coaching, may need an add-on, so confirm what each plan includes
  • Pricing tiers get more layered as your roster grows
  • No cycle or lab data found on its official pages as of September 2026

Who Should Choose This

Trainerize fits coaches and studios planning to grow past a handful of clients who want scheduling and payments handled in the same place as programming. If TrueCoach doesn't have the business tools you need, our Trainerize alternatives roundup covers more options in this same category.

Pricing (as of October 16, 2026): Basic free (1 client), Grow $9/month (2 clients), Pro 5 from $23/month (5-200 clients), Studio Plus from $248/month per location (500+ clients). 30-day free trial, no card required. Trainerize's own FAQ and comparison table show slightly different figures in places (for example Grow at $10 and Pro at $25), so confirm at checkout.

Client app: Fitness App (ABC Trainerize) is free to download and rated 4.90/5 from about 68.7K ratings, with a version dated September 5, 2026.

Learn more: trainerize.com

For AI-Assisted Programming: Everfit

Best if you want: AI help building workouts and meal plans, plus forms-based check-ins for a larger roster. Everfit describes itself as an all-in-one platform for fitness coaches, gyms, studios, and sport coaches, as of September 2026.[3]

Key Features

  • Custom workout builder with an exercise library and an AI Workout Builder, as of September 2026[3]
  • Direct messaging and inbox for client communication
  • Forms and questionnaires for structured check-ins
  • Community forums for group engagement
  • Food journal and macro tracking, with a meal plans and recipe books add-on
  • Habit tracking with automated task reminders
  • Custom branding on Pro, with white-label options at Enterprise

Strengths

  • Free Starter tier covers up to 5 clients with core features
  • Forms and questionnaires make structured, repeatable check-ins easy to set up
  • Community forums support group and cohort-style coaching
  • Scales to enterprise fitness brands with custom pricing and team management

Limitations

  • Some tools, like meal plans and recipe books, are paid add-ons rather than included outright
  • Pricing spans a wide range (Pro from $16/month billed annually, up to $290/month at 300 clients, as of October 2026) depending on client count[3]
  • No cycle or lab data found on its official pages as of September 2026

Who Should Choose This

Everfit is worth a look if you want AI to speed up program and meal-plan building, or you're running group coaching and want forms and community features built in. See our Everfit vs Trainerize comparison for a closer look at how the two stack up if you're deciding between them specifically.

Pricing (as of October 16, 2026): Starter free (up to 5 clients), Pro from $16/month billed annually for 5 clients (rising to $242/month billed annually, or $290 billed monthly, at 300 clients), Studio from $88/month billed annually (50+ clients), Enterprise by quote. Free trial, no card required.[3]

Client app: Everfit - Train smart is free to download and rated 4.72/5 from about 2.7K ratings, with a version dated October 9, 2026.

Learn more: everfit.io

For a Branded App at a Flat Price: PT Distinction

Best if you want: Your own branded coaching app without a separate line-item fee for it, plus AI tools included on every plan. PT Distinction's pricing and per-additional-client fees are consistent with what's published on its own site, as of September 2026.[4]

Key Features

  • AI Program Builder and AI Meal Planner, which PT Distinction lists as included (confirm on its site)
  • Nutrition coaching, assessments, and group training tools
  • Email, SMS, and in-app messaging with pre-scheduling
  • Habit tracking and automated workflows
  • Custom-branded iOS and Android apps (Pro and Master tiers)

Strengths

  • AI programming and meal-planning tools are listed as included rather than sold as add-ons
  • Branded client app comes with the Pro tier instead of a separate fee
  • Flat, predictable monthly pricing with clearly listed per-extra-client costs
  • 1-month free trial you can cancel without being charged

Limitations

  • No permanently free tier, only the 1-month trial
  • The entry Basic tier caps at 3 clients before per-client fees apply
  • Branded app isn't available on the Basic tier
  • No cycle or lab data found on its official pages as of September 2026

Who Should Choose This

PT Distinction fits coaches who want their own branded app from early on and prefer pricing with clearly listed per-client fees.

Pricing (as of October 16, 2026): Basic $19.90/month (3 clients, +$6/extra client), Pro $59.90/month (25 clients, +$2.40/extra), Master $89.90/month (50 clients, +$1.60/extra). 1-month free trial.

Client app: PT Distinction is rated 4.16/5 from about 121 ratings, the smallest rating base of the four, with a version dated September 1, 2026.

Learn more: ptdistinction.com

Which App Fits Which Type of Coach?

Match the tool to the job. TrueCoach fits straightforward program delivery, Trainerize fits growing from one client to a studio, Everfit fits AI-assisted programming, PT Distinction fits a branded app at published per-client rates, and Veya for Professionals fits coaches whose clients already track cycle, sleep and habits. Here's the shorthand version, organized by what you're actually trying to solve:

Choose TrueCoach if:

  • You coach strength and conditioning, bodybuilding, or CrossFit clients
  • You want programming and messaging to work well without configuring a lot of business tools
  • You already use MyFitnessPal or want wearable data synced in

Choose Trainerize if:

  • You're starting with one client and expect to grow into a studio or gym
  • You want scheduling, payments, and product sales in the same platform as programming
  • You want to try the full feature set with a genuinely free single-client tier first

Choose Everfit if:

  • You want AI to speed up building programs and meal plans
  • You run group or cohort coaching and want forms and community forums
  • You're scaling toward a larger roster with team management needs

Choose PT Distinction if:

  • You want your own branded app without an extra line-item fee for it
  • You want AI programming and meal-planning tools listed as included
  • You'd rather have flat, predictable pricing than a wide scaling range

Choose Veya for Professionals if:

  • Your clients already track cycle, sleep, or habit data and you want it in one shared view
  • You want a light, consent-based way to see health context instead of screenshots
  • Your program-delivery needs are simple, or you're already using another platform for that piece

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the client apps for TrueCoach, Trainerize and Everfit rate on the App Store?

On the US App Store in October 2026, Trainerize's client app (Fitness App, ABC Trainerize) is rated 4.90/5 from about 68.7K ratings, TrueCoach is 4.86/5 from about 35.7K, Everfit is 4.72/5 from about 2.7K, and PT Distinction is 4.16/5 from about 121. Ratings reflect client experience of the app, not the coach-side dashboard, so they say little about programming features. Compare plans in Trainerize alternatives.

Do coaching clients need a special app to share health data with a coach?

With Veya for Professionals, a client logs her own data in the free Veya iOS app and chooses what to share with a coach through a consent-based invite. The other four platforms in this guide show no cycle or lab data on their official pages as of September 2026, since they're built around workout programming and check-ins rather than a client's broader health picture. Our guide on what app lets a coach see a client's cycle and sleep data walks through what that consent step looks like and what a coach actually sees on the other side of it.

What's the difference between fitness coaching software and a health-data client portal?

Coaching software like TrueCoach, Trainerize, Everfit, and PT Distinction is built to deliver a program: build workouts, message clients, track habits, and often handle scheduling and payments. A health-data portal like Veya for Professionals is narrower. It's built to surface health context a client already tracks, like cycle phase, sleep, and habit logs, with a lighter program editor rather than a full delivery platform.

Is it worth paying for AI programming or meal-planning tools?

It depends on how much time you currently spend building programs and meal plans from scratch. Everfit offers an AI Workout Builder, and PT Distinction lists AI program and meal-planning tools as included on every plan, while Everfit's meal plans and recipe books are a paid add-on. If you already have templates you reuse, a simpler drag-and-drop builder like TrueCoach's may get you there just as fast without the extra cost.

Can I use more than one of these tools together?

Plenty of coaches do. A common setup is a program-delivery platform like TrueCoach or Trainerize for building and assigning workouts, paired with a lighter tool for anything that platform doesn't cover. Since Veya for Professionals is a client portal rather than a full programming suite, it's designed to sit alongside whichever program-delivery software you're already using, not replace it.

How much does client tracking software cost for a small coaching business?

For a coach starting out with a handful of clients, Trainerize and Everfit both offer free tiers (1 and 5 clients respectively), and Veya for Professionals is free for up to 3 clients. TrueCoach and PT Distinction don't have permanent free tiers, but both offer trial periods (14 days and one month) before their paid plans start at $26.34 and $19.90 a month as listed on October 16, 2026. Costs scale up from there as your roster grows, so it's worth mapping your expected client count against each platform's tiers before committing.
