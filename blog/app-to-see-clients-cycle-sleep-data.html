What App Lets a Coach See Clients' Cycle and Sleep Data?

Mainstream coaching platforms don't show a client's cycle data, and the sleep they sync from wearables isn't tied to it. A consent-based client portal shows both together. Here's what that actually looks like, and where it deliberately stops.

By Go Go Gaia Team • Published August 26, 2026 • 7 min read • For Coaches

Why Coaching Platforms Don't Do This

It's worth being fair to the platforms here, because the gap isn't a failure on their part. TrueCoach and Trainerize were built to solve program delivery: get a workout in front of a client, collect her video or written check-in, track load over time. That's a real job and they do it well. On paid tiers, some sync sleep and recovery data from wearables like Apple Health or WHOOP, but cycle data isn't part of that picture, and the sleep numbers that do come through are never tied to where a client is in her cycle.

So coaches who want that connection end up bridging it manually. A client texts a screenshot from her period app so a coach can see the cycle side at all, then a coach cross-references it by memory against whatever sleep data does come through a wearable integration. It works, sort of, but it's slow and manual, and it depends on her remembering to send something in the first place. Coaching platforms don't build this connection because pairing cycle data with synced sleep is a different product than the one they set out to build.

How Consent-Based Sharing Works

A client portal solves this a different way: instead of a coach requesting data, a client grants it. She sends the invite from her own app using a coach's invite code, chooses what to share, whether that's cycle and sleep, workouts and habits, or labs too, and confirms it. Nothing shows up in a coach's view until she's approved it.

That permission isn't permanent by default. She can revoke it at any time, for any reason, and the coach's access to her data ends immediately. The mechanics are covered in more detail in how sharing and revoking access actually works, but the short version is that the coach never has a standing pipe into her data. Every bit of visibility traces back to a choice she made and can unmake.

What a Coach Actually Sees

Once a client invites a coach in through Go Go Gaia's coach portal, the view includes her current cycle phase, sleep, logged workouts, habit tracking, and any labs she's chosen to share, all in one place instead of scattered across texts and screenshots. A coach can look at whether her energy dips show up in a particular phase, whether her sleep has been shorter than usual the week she's struggled to hit a session, or how consistent her habit logging has been, all without asking her to reconstruct it from memory on a call.

For coaches working the cycle-and-training angle specifically, coaching clients around their menstrual cycle covers how to actually use phase data in programming decisions once you can see it.

What It Deliberately Doesn't Do

This is the part worth being honest about, because it matters for how a coach should use the data. The portal doesn't generate a compliance score or flag missed check-ins. It shows what a client logged, not a judgment about whether she logged enough of it. It doesn't diagnose anything. Cycle phase and symptom patterns show up as data, never as a clinical read on what's happening in her body. It also doesn't pull from a clinic's records or connect to any medical system. Everything a coach sees is what the client logged herself, in her own app, and chose to share.

That last point matters because it sets the boundary of the coaching relationship. A coach reading cycle and sleep data is supporting how a client trains and recovers, not practicing medicine or standing in for her doctor. A coach can keep delivering programs and collecting check-ins exactly how they already do, and layer a client portal on top for the health-data view. Frequently Asked Questions

Does TrueCoach or Trainerize show cycle or sleep data?

Neither shows a client's cycle data, and the sleep data they sync from wearables on paid tiers isn't tied to her cycle phase. TrueCoach and Trainerize handle programming, video check-ins, and workout delivery, which is a different job than connecting cycle and sleep together. Coaches who want that connection usually end up asking clients to screenshot cycle data from a separate tracking app.

Do I need my client's permission to see her cycle and sleep data?

You need her invitation before you see anything at all. She sends you an invite from her own app, chooses what to share, and nothing appears in your view until she confirms it. She can also revoke access at any time, which immediately removes your view of her data.

Can I see a compliance score or workout adherence rating?

The portal doesn't generate compliance scores or adherence ratings. You see her logged workouts, habits, cycle phase, and sleep as she recorded them, without a scoring layer built on top. Judging adherence or missed sessions is a read you make yourself, not one the app produces for you.

Is this a replacement for my coaching software?

It isn't meant to replace program delivery software. Most coaches keep TrueCoach, Trainerize, or whatever they already use for programming and video check-ins, and add a client portal alongside it for the health-data layer those platforms don't cover.
