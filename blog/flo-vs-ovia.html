Flo vs Ovia: Cycle & Pregnancy Compared (2026)

Flo suits you if you want a general cycle tracker with an anonymous community and pregnancy tools. Ovia suits you if you want a kick counter, contraction timer and food-safety lookup, or if your employer or health plan offers it. Veya, our app, suits people who want pregnancy tracked alongside nutrition, mood and sleep.

Quick Answer

Flo and Ovia solve different problems. Flo is a general cycle and pregnancy tracker with Secret Chats and a 24/7 Health Assistant (both Premium, per its App Store listing). Ovia lists a kick counter, contraction timer, food safety lookup and bump tracker, and unlocks premium tools through employer or health-plan coverage. Here's how they compare:

  • If an anonymous community and general cycle tracking matter most: Go with Flo. Secret Chats and a pregnancy mode, with in-depth pregnancy features in Premium.
  • If you want continuity from cycle to pregnancy, plus a kick counter and contraction timer: Go with Ovia. Ovia and Ovia Pregnancy are merged into one app, per its listing.
  • If you want all-in-one health tracking (cycle + mood + sleep + nutrition) for free: Try Veya.

Did Ovia Shut Down or Merge Its Apps?

Ovia did not shut down. Per Ovia's App Store listing, version 7.2.0 (May 27, 2025) merged the Ovia and Ovia Pregnancy apps into one, and the app is now branded Ovia Health by Labcorp. A separate Ovia Parenting & Baby Tracker app is listed under the same developer. Check the App Store release notes for the latest changes.

Some background helps here. Labcorp announced on August 12, 2021 that it had acquired Ovia Health. The App Store listing says users whose employer or health plan covers Ovia can enter their plan information to unlock premium tools such as health coaching and programs for birth control tracking, endometriosis and PCOS.

If you used a standalone Ovia Pregnancy app and were redirected, the listing says former Ovia Pregnancy users can log in with the same email and password. Check current pricing and what is covered inside the app rather than relying on older articles, including older ones of ours. For more on other apps to consider, see our Ovia alternatives guide.

Flo: What You Get

Flo is a general cycle and pregnancy tracker. Its App Store listing claims over 570 million downloads worldwide (as of July 2026) and lists Secret Chats, a 24/7 Health Assistant and in-depth pregnancy features under Premium.

Features

  • Cycle tracking with AI-powered predictions
  • Pregnancy mode for after conception, with checklists and guidance; in-depth pregnancy features are Premium
  • Visual tracking of baby growth and body changes
  • An article library with expert-reviewed content, per Flo
  • 24/7 Health Assistant (Flo Premium)
  • Secret Chats for anonymous community discussions (Premium)
  • Apple Watch support
  • Checklists and guidance after conception

Like any calendar-based tracker, Flo's AI-powered predictions are still built on cycle averages under the hood — our breakdown of why period trackers get predictions wrong covers why that math can miss regardless of which app you're using.

Strengths

An anonymous community. Secret Chats give you anonymous discussions with other users, and the listing places them under Premium. If you have a question at 2am, someone there may have asked it before.

A content library. Flo says its content is reviewed by medical experts before it goes live. If you want to learn while you track, that is a draw.

Pregnancy tools. The listing describes a pregnancy mode for after conception with checklists and guidance; in-depth pregnancy features are listed as Premium.

Limitations

Privacy is the big concern. The FTC announced a proposed settlement with Flo Health on January 13, 2021, alleging it shared health data from millions of users with third parties including Facebook's and Google's analytics divisions, AppsFlyer and Flurry despite privacy promises; the final order came June 22, 2021. A related $59.5 million class settlement (Google $48 million, Flo $8 million, Flurry $3.5 million) received preliminary approval in April 2026, with a final approval hearing on October 29, 2026 (HIPAA Journal). Meta did not settle: a federal jury found on August 4, 2025 that Meta violated the California Invasion of Privacy Act, with damages to be decided separately (PPC Land). Flo has since added Anonymous Mode, rolled out in 2022 and open-sourced in 2023 (Flo Health press page).

Several pregnancy tools sit behind Flo Premium. Per the listing, the Health Assistant, Secret Chats and in-depth pregnancy features need a subscription.

Narrower scope. The listing describes cycle, pregnancy and wellbeing tools and mentions Apple Watch, but not meal logging or other wearables.

Pricing

Free: Core cycle-tracking tools. Premium adds the Health Assistant, Secret Chats and in-depth pregnancy features.

Flo Premium: Flo's App Store listing lists Premium purchases up to $59.99 (as of October 2026), with lower-priced tiers also listed.

Download: Available on iOS and Android

Ovia: What You Get

Ovia is built around a continuous record from cycle tracking into pregnancy. Its App Store listing describes one app combining Ovia and Ovia Pregnancy, with Ovia Parenting & Baby Tracker listed separately.

Features

  • Combined cycle + pregnancy tracker in one app
  • Baby growth calendar, baby names, and hand and foot size comparisons
  • Bump tracker for recording your growing belly
  • Food safety lookup tool (search any food to check if it's safe)
  • Kick counter and contraction timer, included
  • Symptom tracking
  • Premium tools via employer or health plan (health coaching, PCOS and endometriosis programs)

Strengths

One app from cycle to pregnancy. Because Ovia and Ovia Pregnancy are merged, former Ovia Pregnancy users can log in with the same email and password, per the listing.

Kick counter and contraction timer are listed. The App Store listing names both, along with a bump tracker.

A food safety lookup tool. The listing describes a tool to search foods during pregnancy; check its guidance with your care provider.

Limitations

Pricing is unclear for consumers. The App Store listing labels the app Free but does not describe what, if anything, requires payment, and premium tools are tied to employer or health-plan coverage. The listing doesn't show a consumer price.

Community not compared. We did not find a community feature described on Ovia's listing, so we can't compare it with Secret Chats.

Narrower scope. The listing mentions a food safety lookup but not meal logging or wearable integrations.

Owned by Labcorp. Labcorp announced the acquisition on August 12, 2021, and the app is branded Ovia Health by Labcorp. If you get Ovia through an employer or health plan, read the privacy policy and your plan's terms to see how your data is handled.

Pricing

Free: The App Store lists the app as free to download; premium tools unlock through employer or health-plan coverage.

Ovia Cycle & Pregnancy Tracker: The listing shows no in-app purchase prices (as of October 2026), so check current pricing in the app.

Download: Available on iOS and Android

Which App Has Better Community Support for Pregnancy Questions?

Flo's App Store listing describes Secret Chats, anonymous discussions that sit under Premium, plus a 24/7 Health Assistant for quick questions. We did not find a community feature on Ovia's App Store listing, so we can't compare the two on community size or quality.

If an anonymous community and an AI helper matter most, Flo's listing describes both, as Premium features. If you mostly want practical pregnancy tools, Ovia's listing names a kick counter, contraction timer and food safety lookup. For due-date-grouped communities, see BabyCenter and What to Expect, which we cover in our Ovia vs What to Expect vs BabyCenter guide.

Is Flo Premium Worth It vs Ovia's Free Features?

This question needs reframing, because the two apps' pricing is described differently on their App Store listings.

Flo's listing describes core cycle-tracking tools as free, with the Health Assistant, Secret Chats and in-depth pregnancy features listed as Premium. Premium purchases are listed up to $59.99 (as of October 2026).

Ovia's listing labels the app Free without describing what requires payment, and says employer or health-plan coverage unlocks premium tools. The listing doesn't show a consumer price, so check inside the app.

So the practical comparison is Flo's optional Premium upgrade against whatever Ovia premium access you get through an employer or plan. If you're weighing Flo Premium, ask whether the Health Assistant, Secret Chats and in-depth pregnancy features matter enough to pay for. If Ovia's kick counter, contraction timer and food safety lookup are what you want, those are named on the listing; confirm what is included in your version.

Should You Choose Flo, Ovia, or Veya?

There's no single "best" app. It depends on what you care about most. Here's the honest breakdown:

Choose Flo if:

  • You want an anonymous community (Secret Chats, Premium)
  • You want a general tracker with a pregnancy mode for later
  • An article library matters to you
  • You need Android support
  • You're comfortable with Flo's privacy history (or willing to use Anonymous Mode)

Choose Ovia if:

  • You used Ovia for cycle tracking and want to stay in one app into pregnancy
  • A built-in kick counter and contraction timer matter to you
  • The food safety lookup tool is useful to you
  • You have employer or health-plan coverage for it, or are comfortable with the subscription cost
  • Your employer or health plan offers Ovia's premium tools

Choose Veya if:

  • You want pregnancy tracking (kick counter, contraction timer) alongside nutrition, mood, and sleep in one app
  • You track with a wearable (Apple Watch, Oura, or Garmin) and want that data connected to your pregnancy
  • You want a generous free tier with no ads
  • You're on iOS (or okay using the web app on other platforms)
  • Community and editorial content aren't what you're looking for from a tracking app
