TrueCoach Alternatives for Coaches Who Want More Client Context

If you're evaluating TrueCoach alongside other options, this guide covers what TrueCoach does well, why coaches go looking anyway, and how Trainerize, Everfit, PT Distinction, My PT Hub, and Go Go Gaia for Professionals compare on program delivery, pricing, and client data. Quick Answer

TrueCoach is a solid, well-established coach-first platform, and switching away isn't the right move for most coaches who use it. Here's the short version of who else is worth a look:

  • For a permanent free tier and room to scale toward gym size: Trainerize. A free single-client plan and tiers that stretch past TrueCoach's 50-client cap
  • For white-label branding at a lower entry price: Everfit. A free forever tier plus per-client pricing that drops as your roster grows
  • For deeper business and CRM tools: PT Distinction. Built-in assessments, an AI marketing assistant, and automated client workflows
  • For a flatter, unlimited-client price: My PT Hub. Two tiers with no per-client add-on fees once you're past the entry plan
  • For whole-person client context alongside training: Go Go Gaia for Professionals. Cycle phase, sleep, and labs in one consented view. Our app, free for up to 3 clients, with honest limitations
  • If what you actually want is a simple, coach-first setup with a huge video library: TrueCoach itself is worth staying with

TrueCoach: What It Does Well

TrueCoach markets itself as "#1 software for personal trainers"2. It's a coach-first platform built around a drag-and-drop workout builder, a library of more than 3,500 professionally recorded exercise videos with a custom-upload option, in-app messaging with multi-media and group support, and Stripe-based payment processing for one-time or recurring charges2.

Nutrition support comes through a "Documents" feature for delivering meal plans plus a MyFitnessPal integration for macro and calorie tracking2. Wearable data syncs from Apple Health, Garmin, WHOOP, and Oura, as of September 20262. Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial and no credit card required, and first-time annual subscribers get a 90-day money-back guarantee1.

Where it makes sense to look elsewhere is less about quality and more about fit. TrueCoach has no permanent free plan, its pricing steps between three flat client-count tiers rather than scaling more gradually, and some coaches want deeper business or CRM tooling than a pure program-delivery platform offers. Coaches training female clients may also want health context TrueCoach was never built to show, like where a client is in her cycle or how her sleep tracked against a hard training week.

Why Coaches Look for Alternatives

Most coaches who search for TrueCoach alternatives aren't unhappy with it. They're checking whether a permanent free tier, more gradual pricing, deeper business tooling, or a kind of client data TrueCoach doesn't track fits their practice better.

If that last one, cycle, sleep, or lab context, is the piece you're missing, see what the research actually says about training around the menstrual cycle.

The Alternatives Compared

Here are four established program-delivery platforms plus one app that covers a different gap entirely, in no particular order of quality. For a direct head-to-head on two of the biggest names, see our TrueCoach vs Trainerize comparison.

For a Free Tier and Room to Scale: Trainerize

Best if you want: A permanent free plan to start on, and pricing that scales from a single client to a full gym roster.

Key Features

  • AI Workout Builder, drag-and-drop custom programs, a professional video library, native nutrition tools (Smart Meal Planner, Flexible Meal Planner), and habit coaching with streaks and milestone badges4
  • 1-1 and group messaging with automated and voice messages, plus wearable sync with Apple Watch, Withings, Garmin, Fitbit, and MyFitnessPal covering steps, sleep, resting heart rate, weight, body fat, lean mass, blood pressure, and caloric burn, as of September 20264

Strengths

  • A 30-day free trial with unlimited clients and no credit card required, on top of the permanent free Basic plan, as of September 20263
  • More than 400,000 personal trainers and coaches use it, per the company3

Limitations

  • Four pricing tiers tied to client count mean more plan-comparing than TrueCoach's flatter three-tier structure

Pricing (as of September 2026): Free Basic plan for 1 client. Grow $9/month for 2 clients. Pro 5 $23/month, scaling from 5 up to 200 clients. Studio Plus from $248/month base, covering 500 to 1,000-plus clients with custom per-location pricing above that. Annual billing saves 10% across all paid tiers3.

Learn more: trainerize.com

For White-Label Branding at a Lower Price: Everfit

Best if you want: A free forever starting tier and a custom-branded app at a lower price than some competitors charge for the same feature.

Key Features

  • AI-powered programming and AI Workout Builder with sport-specific coaching, skill drills, and performance analytics, plus native nutrition tools (meal plan creation, recipe books, macro tracking, MacroSnap AI meal-photo logging)6
  • Messaging with voice notes, scheduled and automated messages, community forums, broadcast announcements, and client forms or questionnaires6

Strengths

  • 5 clients on the free Starter plan, more room than TrueCoach's trial-only entry5
  • Per-client cost drops as your roster grows across the Pro tier, as of September 20265

Limitations

  • Full white-label branding sits on the Studio and Enterprise tiers, and several features, like automation workflows, on-demand content collections, and meal plans, are separate paid add-ons rather than included5
  • The features page lists a general integrations callout without naming specific wearable platforms, so which devices sync isn't confirmed as of September 20266, and there's no menstrual cycle or hormone context either way

Pricing (as of September 2026): Starter free for up to 5 clients. Pro from $16 to $290/month scaling across 5 to 300 clients. Studio from $88 to $358/month for 50 to 500 clients. Enterprise custom-priced for 500-plus clients. Add-ons like automation, on-demand collections, and meal plans are priced separately5.

Learn more: everfit.io

For Deeper Business and CRM Tools: PT Distinction

Best if you want: A platform that leans into the business side of coaching, not just program delivery, with built-in assessments and automated client workflows.

Key Features

  • A program builder with circuits, supersets, and custom program types, plus technique video review and progress charts8
  • Nutrition tools (photo food diary, nutritionist-made templates, AI Meal Planner, MyFitnessPal integration for goals and macros), plus an AI Program Builder and AI Marketing Assistant on every plan at no extra cost, per the company7

Strengths

  • A 1-month free trial on every tier, cancel anytime with nothing owed, per the company7

Limitations

  • Custom-branded apps are reserved for the Pro and Master tiers, and additional-client fees apply once you pass each tier's included client count7

Pricing (as of September 2026): Basic $19.90/month for up to 3 clients, plus $6/additional client. Pro $59.90/month for up to 25 clients, plus $2.40/additional client. Master $89.90/month for up to 50 clients, plus $1.60/additional client. All tiers include a 1-month free trial7.

Learn more: ptdistinction.com

For a Flatter, Unlimited-Client Ladder: My PT Hub

Best if you want: A simpler two-step price jump where Premium and Ultimate cover unlimited clients instead of the per-client add-on fees TrueCoach and PT Distinction charge.

Key Features

  • Wearable data insights are mentioned as a feature on the pricing page, though the specific devices supported aren't named there, so confirm directly if wearable sync matters to your workflow9

Strengths

  • A 30-day free trial with no card required on every plan9

Limitations

  • We fetched My PT Hub's pricing page for this comparison but not its full features page, so its feature detail here is lighter-touch than the other four. No menstrual cycle, hormone, or lab tracking appears on the pricing page.

Pricing (as of September 2026): Starter $25/month ($22.50/month billed annually) for 3 clients. Premium $59/month ($52/month annual), with an introductory promotional rate for the first two months, for unlimited clients. Ultimate $215/month ($195/month annual) for unlimited clients. All tiers include a 30-day free trial9.

Learn more: mypthub.net

How to Choose, by Coach Type

The right pick depends less on which platform is "best" and more on your practice's stage and gap.

  • Solo trainer starting out: TrueCoach's 14-day trial or Trainerize's free Basic plan are the lowest-friction ways to test a coaching app before committing.
  • Growing practice outgrowing spreadsheets and DMs: Everfit's free forever tier or My PT Hub's flat unlimited-client rate both work well once you're managing more clients but aren't ready for gym-scale software.
  • Established business that wants CRM tools built in: PT Distinction, or Trainerize's higher tiers, cover similar ground at gym scale.
  • Coach whose gap is health context, not program delivery: Keep your current platform and add the Go Go Gaia coach portal alongside it. Our guide to client tracking apps for coaches compares the whole category.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best alternative to TrueCoach?

There isn't one best alternative, since each app fits a different practice. Trainerize suits a permanent free tier and room to scale toward gym size. Everfit suits white-label branding without Trainerize's higher-tier price. PT Distinction leans into business and CRM tools. My PT Hub suits a flat rate over per-client fees. Go Go Gaia for Professionals suits coaches whose gap is a client's cycle, sleep, and lab data, not program delivery.

Is TrueCoach free?

TrueCoach has no permanent free plan for coaches, though clients use it at no cost to them. Coaches get a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, and paid plans start around $26.34 a month (billed annually) for up to 5 active clients, as of September 20261.

What's the difference between TrueCoach and Trainerize?

Both are established platforms with workout builders, messaging, and habit tracking, but they scale differently. TrueCoach has three flat client-cap tiers (5, 20, or 50) and no permanent free plan. Trainerize has a free single-client Basic plan plus paid tiers that scale from 2 clients past 1,000 in smaller price steps. Our TrueCoach vs Trainerize guide covers this in more depth.

Do any TrueCoach alternatives track a client's menstrual cycle?

Not the program-delivery platforms here. TrueCoach, Trainerize, Everfit, PT Distinction, and My PT Hub are built around workout programming and client communication, with no menstrual cycle tracking for the coach to see. Go Go Gaia for Professionals shows cycle phase alongside sleep, workout and habit logs, and labs, but it isn't a program-delivery platform, so some coaches run it alongside one of the others rather than instead of it.

Is My PT Hub cheaper than TrueCoach?

It depends on your client count. My PT Hub's Starter plan is $25 a month for 3 clients, close to TrueCoach's $26.34 for 5. But My PT Hub's Premium and Ultimate tiers include unlimited clients for a flat rate, while TrueCoach keeps climbing through its 20- and 50-client tiers, so a larger roster tends to cost less on My PT Hub, as of September 2026. Confirm current pricing directly, since both use promotional rates that can change.

Can I switch from TrueCoach without losing my client data?

Most of these platforms let you export workout history and client lists in some form, but there's no universal one-click migration between coaching apps. Check each platform's export and onboarding documentation before you commit, and plan on a short overlap while you rebuild program templates in the new tool.
