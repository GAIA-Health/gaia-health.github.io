## 2. The format-vetting method (Salim's rules, adopted)

Before cloning any format:
- The account must have a **wall of consistent bangers** on the same skeleton — one viral post is luck, not a format. Never clone a one-hit profile.
- Look for all four at once: high views, strong engagement, active comments, recent post date.
- Reverse-engineer the DNA: hook on slide 1, slide count, where the value lands, what earns the save. Then rebuild it with our content inside, don't screenshot-copy.
- Validate niche demand with TikTok search insights when picking new sub-niches.
