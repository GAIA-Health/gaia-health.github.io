Built for tracking without a period

Log by date, not by period

Log mood, energy, skin, sleep, and other symptoms by date. The calendar doesn't need a period to organize around, so the record keeps building either way.

Wearable signals fill the gap

Temperature, sleep, and HRV come in automatically, so you have a daily signal whether or not you bleed. On iPhone, Apple Watch, Oura, WHOOP and Garmin data arrives through Apple Health. In the web app, Oura and Withings connect directly.

Log your birth-control method

Record the method you use, such as the pill, an IUD, an implant, a patch, a ring, an injection, or condoms, so your tracking has that context.

Patterns across weeks

Veya correlates what you log, like how poor sleep lines up with a harder day, so you can see your own patterns without a period date.

Your history stays with you

If you change method or stop, your history stays in Veya, so you can see what shifted and when.

A record for your appointments

Export an organized record of your symptoms, mood, and sleep to bring to your care team.

See how it all connects

When bleeding isn't there to mark the start of a cycle, other signals do the work. Veya keeps your symptoms, mood, energy, skin, sleep, and wearable temperature on one timeline, and looks for patterns across them, like which weeks your sleep dips or your mood shifts. It shows you the pattern and doesn't tell you what it means. That conversation is yours to have with your clinician.
